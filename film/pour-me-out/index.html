Opening storyboard: SH010 "Arrival at the stool"

One locked wide camera, one ground line, one stool position. The camera does not cut until KC is singing. The push-in on the first line is a crop of the same art, so it needs no new drawings. KC enters frame left and walks right. That way he finishes three-quarters right, matching the seated drawings. Staging follows Key Pose Study 001.

Open questions

  1. Local audio file. I need the official release as a local file, ideally the WAV master or a high-quality AAC or MP3 (48 kHz is fine). Save it as film/pour-me-out/audio/pour-me-out.wav. Git ignores that folder, so the recording stays out of history.
  2. Is the intro long enough for the walk? Walk, turn, sit, settle and breathe takes several seconds of screen time. If the intro is short, the walk can start over a few seconds of room tone before the first note, or the empty-stool hold can be cut.
  3. Lyric imagery. Each section's scene note says where lyric-driven imagery could go. Decide that after a listen with the lyrics in front of us, not before.
  4. Ending. Proposed: he finishes, stands, and walks off frame right, leaving the empty stool. The exit reuses the entrance walk drawings. Alternative: hold on him seated and fade.
  5. Aspect ratio. Boards are drawn 16:9. Say so if the release is vertical (9:16) or square instead.
